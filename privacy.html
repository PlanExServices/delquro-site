Commonwealth: Connect

This Privacy Policy explains how Delquro Labs collects, uses, stores, and discloses information in connection with Commonwealth: Connect.

Effective Date: May 25, 2026

1. Overview

Commonwealth: Connect (“the App”) is operated by Delquro Labs (“Delquro Labs,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and disclose information in connection with the App and related services.

The App is intended for use by authorized veterinary hospital teams and clinical staff and is not a general consumer-facing application.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at info@delquro.com.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how the App is used, we may collect the following categories of information:

Account Information
This may include your name, email address, account credentials, role, and organization or facility affiliation.

User Content
This may include chat messages, posts, schedule images, PTO-related submissions, manager-uploaded content, attachments, and other information submitted through the App.

Device and Technical Information
This may include device type, operating system, browser type, app version, IP address, push notification token, and diagnostic or log data.

Usage Information
This may include information about how users access and interact with the App, such as login activity, feature usage, and error events.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through the App to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the App;
  • Authenticate users and manage access to accounts and features;
  • Enable communication, scheduling visibility, PTO requests, and internal updates;
  • Send service-related notifications and operational messages;
  • Respond to support requests and troubleshoot issues;
  • Protect the security, integrity, and reliability of the App;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our policies and terms.

4. How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell personal information.

We may disclose information in the following circumstances:

  • Within your organization: information submitted in the App may be visible to authorized users, administrators, or managers, depending on account roles and feature settings.
  • Service providers: we may use vendors and service providers to support hosting, authentication, storage, notifications, diagnostics, support, or app operations. These providers may process data on our behalf as necessary to operate and support the App.
  • Legal or security reasons: where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, enforce our rights, protect users, or investigate misuse or security issues.
  • Business transfers: if permitted by law, information may be transferred in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

We do not use third-party advertising networks.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies in connection with our website and related online services. Cookies are small text files or similar tools that help a website or service function, remember preferences, understand usage, and support security and performance.

Strictly Necessary Cookies. Some cookies may be required for core website or service functionality, such as security, session management, load balancing, or remembering a user’s privacy choices. Where permitted by applicable law, these cookies may be used without prior consent because they are necessary to provide the service requested by the user.

Non-Essential Cookies. We may also use non-essential cookies or similar technologies for functionality, performance, analytics, or other service improvement purposes. Where required by applicable law, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

Analytics and Measurement. If analytics or audience measurement technologies are used, they will be used only in accordance with applicable law. In some jurisdictions, certain narrowly limited analytics tools may be exempt from consent requirements when they are configured solely for restricted audience measurement and include appropriate safeguards.

Advertising Cookies. We do not use third-party advertising networks in connection with Commonwealth: Connect.

Your Choices. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may be able to control cookies through browser settings, device settings, consent tools, or privacy preference signals where supported. Disabling certain cookies may affect the availability or functionality of some parts of a website or service.

California Notice. If cookies or similar technologies are used in a way that involves personal information subject to California law, California residents may have rights to know, delete, correct, or opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, subject to applicable law.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain operational records, support legitimate business needs, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will delete it, de-identify it, or securely dispose of it within a commercially reasonable timeframe, subject to backup retention, technical limitations, legal obligations, or legitimate audit and security needs.

Target timeframe for verified deletion requests: 30 days.

7. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of your account or personal information, contact info@delquro.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.

We may take reasonable steps to verify your identity and authority before processing a request. Some information may be retained as required for legal, security, audit, backup, or operational reasons.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children’s Privacy

Commonwealth: Connect is intended for workplace and team operational use and is not directed to children.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing, subject to applicable law.

To make a privacy-related request, contact info@delquro.com.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the Effective Date above. Continued use of the App after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective means the updated policy will apply going forward.
