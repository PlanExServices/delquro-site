Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Delquro Labs handles information for its website, software, and applications, including Commonwealth: Connect.

Information We May Collect

Depending on how the software is used, we may collect or process:

  • Account information such as name, email address, login credentials, role, or facility association.
  • Team communication content, including messages, announcements, and operational updates.
  • Scheduling-related content, PTO-related content, huddle content, uploaded images, and manager-posted information.
  • Support requests and communications sent to Delquro Labs.
  • Technical data such as device type, browser, app version, error logs, security logs, and notification tokens.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the software.
  • Support team communication and operational coordination.
  • Send notifications and service-related messages.
  • Provide support and troubleshoot issues.
  • Protect the security, reliability, and integrity of the service.
  • Improve usability, performance, and future product features.

How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only as needed to operate the service, comply with law, protect users, or work with service providers that help provide hosting, authentication, storage, notifications, analytics, security, or support.

Facility and Administrator Access

Commonwealth: Connect is designed for workplace team use. Certain information may be visible to authorized users, managers, administrators, or facility leadership depending on permissions and product configuration.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, and support legitimate operational needs. Retention periods may vary by data type and facility configuration.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we work to protect data in a manner appropriate to the nature of the information handled.

Privacy Requests

Users may request access, correction, deletion, or other privacy-related assistance by contacting Delquro Labs. Some requests may require identity verification, administrator approval, or retention of limited information where required by law or operational need.

Children

Delquro Labs software is intended for workplace and organizational use. It is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as products, laws, or business practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

Questions or requests can be sent to info@delquro.com.
