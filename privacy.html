Commonwealth: Connect

This Privacy Policy explains how Delquro Labs collects, uses, stores, and discloses information in connection with Commonwealth: Connect.

Effective date: [Month Day, Year]

1. Who We Are

Commonwealth: Connect (“the App”) is operated by Delquro Labs (“Delquro Labs,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, contact us at info@delquro.cominfo@delquro.com.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how the App is used, we may collect the following categories of information:

Account Information
This may include your name, email address, account credentials, user role, facility association, and other account setup information.

Team Communications and User Content
This may include chat messages, huddle posts, schedule images, PTO-related submissions, file uploads, and other content submitted through the App.

Device and Technical Information
This may include device type, operating system, browser type, app version, IP address, push notification token, and basic diagnostic or log information.

Usage Information
This may include information about how users interact with the App, such as feature usage, login activity, or error events.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through the App to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the App;
  • Authenticate users and manage account access;
  • Enable communication, scheduling visibility, PTO requests, and huddle distribution;
  • Send operational messages, alerts, and notifications;
  • Respond to support requests and troubleshoot issues;
  • Protect the security, integrity, and reliability of the App;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies.

4. How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Within your organization: information you submit in the App may be visible to authorized users, managers, or administrators of your facility or organization, depending on the feature and permissions involved.
  • Service providers: we may use third-party vendors or infrastructure providers to help operate the App, such as hosting, authentication, storage, notifications, diagnostics, or support tools.
  • Legal or security reasons: where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, enforce our rights, protect users, or prevent fraud, abuse, or security issues.
  • Business transfers: if permitted by law and applicable contract obligations, information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain operational records, fulfill legitimate business needs, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

When information is no longer needed, we will delete it, de-identify it, or securely dispose of it within a commercially reasonable timeframe, subject to backup retention, technical limitations, legal obligations, or legitimate security and audit needs.

Internal target deletion timeline after verified request: [e.g., 30 days].

6. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of your account or personal information, contact info@delquro.cominfo@delquro.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.

We may take reasonable steps to verify your identity and authority before processing the request. Some information may be retained as required for security, legal, audit, backup, or operational continuity purposes.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s Privacy

Commonwealth: Connect is intended for workplace and team operational use and is not directed to children.

9. International and Regional Privacy Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, or restriction of certain personal information, subject to applicable law.

To make a privacy-related request, contact info@delquro.cominfo@delquro.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the App after an updated policy becomes effective means the updated policy will apply going forward.
