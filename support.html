Support

For help with Delquro Labs software or Commonwealth: Connect, contact support using the email below.

Contact

Email: info@delquro.com

Helpful Details to Include

  • The application you are using.
  • Your name, email address, and organization or facility if applicable.
  • The device, operating system, browser, or app version.
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected to happen.
  • Screenshots or error messages if they help explain the issue.

Privacy and Account Requests

Privacy questions, account deletion requests, or data requests can also be sent to info@delquro.com. For account deletion details, visit the Account Deletion page.

Response Time

Delquro Labs will make reasonable efforts to respond to support requests as soon as practical. Response times may vary based on the nature of the request and product stage.
